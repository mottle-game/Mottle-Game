## 🎮 What is Mottle Game?

**Mottle** is a minimalist stealth-survival arcade game. You play as an organism navigating a vibrant garden floor under the sweep of hostile **Seeker** searchlights.

Your goal is simple: **Collect all glowing fireflies while avoiding detection.**

The twist? **You can only hide when you stop moving.** When you travel, you shine in high-contrast coral. The moment you let go of the controls, you melt seamlessly into the background pattern beneath your feet.

## 🧠 Key Mechanics Every Player Should Know

### 1. Camouflage Takes a Fraction of a Second
Camouflage is not instant—it takes a moment to settle after you halt. If you wait until a light cone is already touching you to let go, you will be caught. **Release half a beat early.**

### 2. The Alert Meter
- When a Seeker's light touches you while you are moving or unblended, the **Alert Meter** climbs rapidly.
- Once the meter fills completely, your run ends.
- Staying fully still under cover slowly cools down partial alert buildup.

### 3. Seekers Have Patterns
Seekers move in predictable paths and angles. Learn to watch their sweep rhythms rather than keeping your eyes glued exclusively to your own character.

### 4. Movement Creates Contrast
The garden floor has varied patches and hues. You don't have to match colors manually—the game adapts your skin to whatever patch you stop on. All you need to do is **stay completely still**.

## 💡 Top Survival Strategies

- ⏳ **Sprint in Bursts**: Never make long, continuous cross-arena journeys. Travel from one safe pause-point to another.
- 🧘 **Patience Over Panic**: When a light beam bears down on you, resist the instinct to run. Releasing and freezing is almost always the safer move.
- 🗺️ **Pick Your Route**: Grab isolated fireflies during quiet sweep intervals, and leave cluster targets for when the main lights pivot away.
- 📱 **Mobile Play Tip**: Keep your thumb inside the arena frame so your browser doesn't accidentally trigger page scrolls or gestures.
